## Capture and editing decisions

- Browser views come from https://gridlines-beta.vercel.app/, captured through the browser tool at a requested 1920 × 1080 viewport. Returned images are 1905 × 1072; the final canvas is 1920 × 1080.
- 760 genuine browser frames include navigation and drill-down transitions. Captured UI states are held during explanation. This is an edited, frame-based screen demonstration, not a continuous recording of a live presentation.
- Source panels render exact excerpts from repository commit `00a6cd043021be32e44cd5977700c5e6fd15e43b`, with paths and line numbers. They are readable source excerpts, not a recording of an IDE session.
- Green and neutral colors follow the deployed product. No stock footage, generated website screens, music, or personal presenter is used.
- Captions remain separate in SRT and are also burned into the video. Sentence boundaries follow separately synthesized sentences; timing within a split sentence is estimated.
- Brief AI-assistance disclosure appears in the closing narration and on screen. No claim is made that this is an unedited live recording.

## Presenter option

No setup is required for this cut. A future likeness-based version would require the user's supplied portrait or consent footage and an approved provider workflow. Do not substitute an unrelated face. Do not upload personal media without naming the receiving service and obtaining authorization.
